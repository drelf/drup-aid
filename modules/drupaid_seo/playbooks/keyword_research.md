# Playbook: Keyword research + competitive gap analysis

Full keyword *volume* data needs a paid tool (SEMrush/Ahrefs) or estimates; but you can do real, free research from Google Search Console + SERP analysis. Guide the user and produce the recommendations.

## Free-first method
1. **Mine GSC for what already works**: pull Search Console queries (Performance report, or via API once connected). Find queries where the site ranks positions 5–20 with impressions but low CTR — these are "striking distance" wins: improving the page can move them onto page one fast.
2. **Seed expansion**: take the top 5 commercial-intent topics and expand with Google autocomplete, "People also ask", and "related searches" at the bottom of the SERP.
3. **Group by intent**: sort candidates into informational / commercial / transactional. Map each cluster to one target page (pillar) + supporting pages (cluster).

## Competitive gap analysis
4. Identify 2–3 direct competitors. For each, look at the pages ranking for your target terms.
5. **Gap = terms competitors rank for that you don't.** Free signal: search your target terms, note which competitor pages appear, and what subtopics they cover that your page omits. (Paid tools automate this with a "Keyword Gap" report — recommend if budget allows.)
6. Prioritize gaps by: commercial intent × achievable difficulty × business fit.

## Output to hand the user
- A ranked keyword list grouped into pillar→cluster, each row: keyword, intent, current position (if any), target page, and the action (new page / optimize existing / add section).
- The top 3 "striking distance" quick wins to do first.
